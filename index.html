✅ Project complete (17 November 2026)

  • In January 2026, 38 smileys were SVG and 26 were still animated GIFs. We converted three of them ourselves: yes, no and lol.
  • On 26 October 2026, Tweakers released SVG versions of the remaining 26 in one deployment (23 of them animated with CSS), so all 64 smileys are now vector. Developer XWB announced it on the forum six minutes later: "We hebben dit project uit de koelkast gehaald en de resterende smileys omgezet." Shiny and worshippy were updated to the same style the next day.
  • No smileys were added or removed. Only shiny and worshippy changed among the older SVGs, and only in how their CSS is written. They look the same.
